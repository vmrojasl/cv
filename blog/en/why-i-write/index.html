Why I write about science, capital and execution

A working notebook for translating between the languages that keep research apart from companies, deals and investment.

Victor Manuel Rojas Leal·October 20, 2026·1 min read

Leer en Español
Share

For the last few years I have worked where research, intellectual property, market, finance and execution meet. At that junction I have seen the same pattern again and again: the science is good, but it never becomes a company, a licence or an investment, because every party speaks a different language.

This blog is my working notebook for translating between those languages.

#What you will find here

  • Technology transfer: how a deal is structured, what each side negotiates and where it usually breaks.
  • Science spin-offs: governance, valuation and capital readiness.
  • Technology valuation: ranges, assumptions and negotiation anchors.
  • Ecosystems: universities, companies, investors and public institutions moving in the same direction.

#How I write

I write from real cases, without disclosing confidential information. Every article will try to leave you something usable: a framework, a checklist or a question for your next board meeting.

Key point

Complexity becomes valuable only when it can be translated into a decision, a deal and a path to scale.

If you want to suggest a topic or discuss a case, write to me at vmrlbiz@gmail.com or on LinkedIn.
