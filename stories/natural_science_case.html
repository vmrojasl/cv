Turning Colombian biodiversity science into investable venture value

Ki Natural Science presents a science-based innovation platform that converts botanical knowledge, clean technologies and biobased product development into solutions for industry, communities and territories.

The work focused on translating a broad scientific and technology portfolio into an investor-ready narrative: venture thesis, value story, strategic assets, partner logic and scalable market pathways.

Case snapshot
TORCH Cold botanical extraction technology designed for cleaner, standardized industrial transfer.
ESPORA Controlled cultivation device for functional mushrooms with autonomous environmental management.
Biobased Product research spanning botanical beverages, ingredients, aromas and functional experiences.
Territory Technology transfer and training with communities, including the Wila ancestral reserve in Cauca.

Challenge

The venture challenge

The company had more than a product story. It had science, prototypes, intellectual assets, sustainability claims, regulated-market opportunities, community transfer and multiple possible commercialization routes.

Strategic complexity Many assets, one investment-grade story

Organized technologies, services and products into a coherent platform thesis for capital and partners.

Market access From lab language to buyer logic

Capital readiness Valuation with strategic narrative

Connected intangible assets, growth options and execution milestones to a credible investor conversation.

Role

Victor's role in the case

The contribution was not just advisory language. It was venture structuring: making the business legible to investors, institutions and strategic partners while preserving the scientific and sustainability core.

Venture thesis Defined the platform logic

Positioned KNS as a science venture platform across extraction, cultivation, biobased products and ERSA advisory.

Asset framing Translated science into strategic value

Deal narrative Prepared the capital conversation
